# KPI Dictionary

## Lead Volume
Number of customer inquiries registered during a defined period.

## Qualified Lead Rate
Qualified leads divided by total registered inquiries.

## Follow-Up Compliance
Percentage of leads receiving the required follow-up within the expected time window.

## Conversion Rate
Converted leads divided by qualified leads or total leads, depending on the reporting view.

## Source Effectiveness
Lead quality and conversion performance by acquisition source or campaign.

## Service Demand
Inquiry volume by service category and destination.

## Lost Lead Reasons
Distribution of documented reasons for non-conversion.

## Customer Satisfaction
Results from post-service or post-inquiry satisfaction activities.

## Reporting Principle

A KPI was included only when it supported a concrete operational or management decision.
