# Business Value Framework

## Revenue and Conversion
Better visibility and follow-up can reduce lost opportunities and improve conversion discipline.

## Operational Efficiency
Structured workflows reduce manual coordination and make ownership clearer.

## Marketing Effectiveness
Source attribution connects acquisition activity with qualified leads and conversion outcomes.

## Customer Experience
Consistent follow-up, complaint handling, and satisfaction measurement create a more controlled customer journey.

## Management Visibility
Shared KPIs improve decision-making and reduce dependence on manually assembled reports.

## Strategic Value

The CRM creates a reusable operational data layer that can support future automation, analytics, and system integration.
