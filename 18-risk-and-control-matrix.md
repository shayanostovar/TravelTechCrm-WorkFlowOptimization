# Risk and Control Matrix

| Risk | Business Impact | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incomplete lead registration | Missing pipeline visibility | Required fields and workflow rules |
| Duplicate customers | Fragmented history and inaccurate reporting | Search and duplicate-check process |
| Missed follow-ups | Lost sales opportunities | Activities, reminders, and escalation |
| Incorrect source attribution | Misleading marketing analysis | Controlled source values and validation |
| Poor adoption | CRM becomes an incomplete secondary system | Training, process ownership, and management use |
| Excessive customization | High maintenance cost | Prefer configuration and justified extensions |
| Weak reporting definitions | Conflicting management numbers | Shared KPI definitions |

## Governance Principle

Controls were designed to improve process reliability without creating unnecessary operational friction.
