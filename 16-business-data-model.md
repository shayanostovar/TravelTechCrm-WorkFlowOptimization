# Business Data Model

## Core Business Entities

### Customer
Identity and contact information for a person or organization interacting with the travel agency.

### Inquiry / Lead
A potential business opportunity associated with a customer, service category, destination, source, and status.

### Activity
Calls, follow-ups, reminders, notes, and other actions associated with a customer or lead.

### Campaign / Source
The marketing or acquisition channel that generated an inquiry.

### Service
Travel service category such as airline ticket, tour package, or visa service.

### Conversion
The outcome of a lead, including successful conversion or a documented loss reason.

### Satisfaction / Complaint
Post-interaction feedback used to monitor service quality and operational issues.

## Data Quality Principle

Reporting quality is limited by capture quality. Required fields and workflow rules were therefore designed around the decisions the organization needed to make.
