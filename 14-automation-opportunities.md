# Automation Opportunities

The project identified repetitive steps where automation could reduce manual work and improve data quality.

## Candidate Automations

- Automatic caller lookup from PBX events.
- Lead creation support for new inbound callers.
- Follow-up reminders based on lead status.
- Escalation for overdue activities.
- Duplicate-customer detection.
- Automatic campaign/source attribution where reliable.
- Satisfaction follow-up after completed services.
- Management alerts for abnormal lead or conversion patterns.
- Scheduled KPI reporting.

## Automation Principle

Automation was used to support a defined business process, not to replace process design. A poorly defined workflow becomes harder to manage when automated.
