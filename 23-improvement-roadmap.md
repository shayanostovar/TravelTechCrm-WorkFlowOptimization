# Improvement Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Process Foundation
- Standardize lead registration.
- Define qualification rules.
- Assign ownership.
- Establish follow-up discipline.

## Phase 2 — Integration
- Connect telephony and digital acquisition sources.
- Reduce duplicate data entry.
- Improve customer identification.

## Phase 3 — Measurement
- Define KPI dictionary.
- Build management reporting.
- Track conversion, source effectiveness, and service demand.

## Phase 4 — Automation
- Add reminders and escalations.
- Automate repeatable workflow steps.
- Introduce scheduled reporting and alerts.

## Phase 5 — Continuous Improvement
- Review adoption.
- Remove unnecessary steps.
- Improve data quality.
- Reassess platform fit and integration needs.
