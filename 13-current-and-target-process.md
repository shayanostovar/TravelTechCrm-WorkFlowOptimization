# Current and Target Process

## Before the CRM Initiative

Customer inquiries entered the organization through multiple channels. Registration and follow-up depended heavily on individuals, which made it difficult to measure lead volume, ownership, conversion, or marketing effectiveness.

## Target Operating Model

1. Capture the inquiry.
2. Identify source and service category.
3. Create or match the customer record.
4. Qualify the lead.
5. Assign ownership.
6. Schedule and execute follow-up.
7. Record outcome and conversion.
8. Trigger post-sale or post-inquiry satisfaction activity.
9. Aggregate results into management reporting.

## Business Impact

The target process converted disconnected customer interactions into a measurable lifecycle. This created a shared operating language between marketing, sales, customer service, management, and IT.
