# Change Management

CRM adoption depends on operational behavior as much as software configuration.

## Key Change Activities

- Explain why the process is changing.
- Define clear responsibilities for lead registration and follow-up.
- Train users around real operational scenarios.
- Keep required data focused on business value.
- Use management reporting from the CRM so users see that the data matters.
- Review recurring user friction and remove unnecessary steps.
- Reinforce ownership through supervisors and team leads.

## Lesson

Users are more likely to adopt a CRM when it helps them complete work, not when it exists only for management reporting.
