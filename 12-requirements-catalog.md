# Requirements Catalog

## Core Functional Requirements

- Register customer inquiries from multiple acquisition channels.
- Capture service type, destination, source, contact information, and inquiry status.
- Assign ownership to sales or reservation staff.
- Track follow-up activities and next actions.
- Support service-specific lead qualification.
- Record conversion outcomes and reasons for loss.
- Track customer satisfaction and complaints.
- Provide management reporting by channel, service, destination, team, and outcome.

## Integration Requirements

- Connect inbound telephony with CRM customer context.
- Reduce duplicate customer records.
- Support data exchange with downstream operational systems where practical.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Consistent data entry.
- Role-appropriate access.
- Auditable workflow history.
- Reliable reporting.
- Maintainable configuration.
- Low operational friction for front-line users.

## Prioritization Principle

Requirements were prioritized by their impact on customer visibility, process consistency, conversion measurement, and management decision-making.
