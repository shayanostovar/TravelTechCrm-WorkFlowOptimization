# CRM Governance

## Ownership

A CRM process requires both business and technical ownership.

### Business Ownership
- Defines workflow rules.
- Owns required data and KPI definitions.
- Reviews adoption and operational performance.
- Prioritizes improvements.

### Technical Ownership
- Maintains configuration and integrations.
- Manages access and reliability.
- Supports data quality controls.
- Implements approved changes.

## Change Control

Changes to fields, stages, automation, integrations, and reports should be assessed for business impact before implementation.

## Governance Goal

Keep the CRM aligned with the operating model as business processes evolve.
