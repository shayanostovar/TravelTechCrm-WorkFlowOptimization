# Glossary

**CRM** — Customer Relationship Management system and associated operating processes.

**Lead** — A customer inquiry with potential commercial value.

**Lead Qualification** — Process of determining whether an inquiry should progress through the sales workflow.

**Conversion** — Successful transition from a lead to a completed commercial outcome.

**Follow-Up** — Planned activity used to continue engagement with a lead or customer.

**Source Attribution** — Identification of the marketing or acquisition source associated with an inquiry.

**Happy Call** — Structured post-interaction follow-up used to measure satisfaction and identify issues.

**KPI** — Key Performance Indicator used to measure a defined business outcome or process.

**PBX** — Private Branch Exchange; in this project, Issabel supported telephony integration.

**Process Owner** — Business role accountable for the effectiveness and evolution of a workflow.
