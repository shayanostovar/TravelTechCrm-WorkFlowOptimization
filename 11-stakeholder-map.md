# Stakeholder Map

## Purpose

This document identifies the major stakeholder groups involved in the CRM workflow optimization initiative and the business concerns that shaped the solution.

## Stakeholder Groups

### Executive Management
- Required visibility into lead generation, conversion, service demand, and customer satisfaction.
- Needed decision-ready reporting rather than fragmented operational data.

### Sales and Reservation Teams
- Needed a consistent way to register, qualify, follow up, and convert customer inquiries.
- Required clear ownership of leads and follow-up activities.

### Marketing
- Needed attribution between campaigns, inquiry sources, qualified leads, and final conversions.
- Required segmentation and campaign-performance feedback.

### Call Center
- Needed caller identification, CRM context, and efficient lead creation from inbound calls.
- Required integration between Issabel PBX and CRM workflows.

### Customer Service
- Needed structured post-sale follow-up, satisfaction measurement, and complaint handling.

### IT and Systems
- Responsible for CRM configuration, telephony integration, data quality, permissions, and operational reliability.

## Business Analysis View

The CRM initiative was treated as a cross-functional operating model rather than a standalone software deployment. Requirements were prioritized based on business value, process impact, data quality, and management visibility.
