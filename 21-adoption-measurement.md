# Adoption Measurement

System deployment does not prove process adoption. Adoption was assessed through operational signals.

## Useful Adoption Indicators

- Percentage of inquiries registered in CRM.
- Percentage of active leads with an owner.
- Percentage of leads with a next action.
- Follow-up completion rate.
- Percentage of closed leads with an outcome reason.
- Number of duplicate or incomplete records.
- Frequency of management reports generated from CRM data.
- User feedback on workflow friction.

## Interpretation

Adoption metrics help distinguish software problems from process, training, ownership, or data-quality problems.
